03 / Methodology

A transparent model with a live check.

The headline is an ordinary least squares regression, and every filter is recomputed in the browser from the underlying country-year rows.

lifeExp = beta0 + beta1 * log10(gdpPercap) + error
  1. Model

    lifeExp = beta0 + beta1 * log10(gdpPercap) + error, fit by ordinary least squares because income is heavily right-skewed.

  2. Inference

    The slope gets a t-distribution based 95% confidence interval and a two-sided p-value.

  3. Client-side recomputation

    Changing a filter refits the model from scratch with the closed-form normal equations. No filtered answer is looked up.

04 / Limitations

What the data cannot tell us.

  • Observational, not experimental. No country was randomly assigned an income level, so reverse causation and confounding remain possible.
  • Country-level aggregation. GDP per capita and life expectancy are national averages, not measures of within-country inequality.
  • Five-year sampling. The data are recorded at five-year intervals from 1952 to 2007, so short-term shocks are invisible.
  • Historical window. The series predates recent shifts in global health and income and should not be extrapolated to the present.
  • Coarse continent grouping. Each continent blends very different national trajectories.
  • Small-sample subsets. A narrow selection can leave few observations. The live n and confidence interval show that instability.
